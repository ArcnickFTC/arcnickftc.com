Community

ARCHNIK is FTC Team 35849. We compete in the FIRST Tech Challenge — a global robotics program where students build and program robots to score points in alliance-based matches.

Our philosophy is simple: NO LIMITS. We push hardware, software, and ourselves past what seems possible.
